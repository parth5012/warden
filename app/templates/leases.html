Zero-Ambient-Authority Capability Model

In the Aegis security architecture, agents possess no persistent elevated permissions. Operations requiring escalated access (e.g. database schema changes, restricted filesystem writes) require an explicit, time-decaying Ephemeral Lease.
When a lease expires or its operations budget is exhausted, the GuardedExecutor seam automatically returns a terminal DENY or halts with a gate_requested interrupt.
